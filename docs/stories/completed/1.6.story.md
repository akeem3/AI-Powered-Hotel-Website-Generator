# Story 1.6: Contact Page with Basic Form

> **Epic:** Epic 1 - Core Hotel Website Infrastructure
> **Status:** Done
> **Priority:** Medium
> **Story Points:** 3
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Created:** 2025-10-20

## 1. The "Why" (Rationale)
This story provides essential contact functionality that enables:

- Customer communication and lead generation
- Business inquiry capture for corporate clients
- Professional presence and trust building
- Form validation patterns for LLM reference
- Foundation for future contact form enhancements

## 2. The "What" (Description)
**As a** potential customer or business partner interested in The Sterling Executive hotel,
**I want** to access a contact page with hotel information and an inquiry form,
**So that** I can easily reach out with questions, booking inquiries, or business proposals.

### AC7: ZOD Contract Implementation for Contact Forms

- [x] Create ZOD schema for ContactForm component:
  ```typescript
  export const ContactFormContract = z.object({
    name: z.string().min(2).max(100),
    email: z.string().email(),
    phone: z.string().regex(/^[+]?[\d\s\-()]+$/).optional(),
    subject: z.string().min(5).max(200),
    message: z.string().min(10).max(2000),
    inquiryType: z.enum(['general', 'booking', 'business', 'complaint']).default('general'),
    subscribe: z.boolean().default(false),
  });
  ```
- [x] Create ZOD schema for ContactInfo component:
  ```typescript
  export const ContactInfoContract = z.object({
    hotelName: z.string().min(1),
    address: z.string().min(1),
    phone: z.string(),
    email: z.string().email(),
    website: z.string().url().optional(),
    hours: z.object({
      weekdays: z.string(),
      weekends: z.string(),
    }).optional(),
  });
  ```
- [x] Add runtime validation to form submission
